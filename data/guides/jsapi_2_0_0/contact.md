# Contact

<p class="bemap-tagline">Questions, ACL provisioning, custom geoservers, EV catalogue additions, bug reports — here's how to reach BeNomad.</p>

## Get in touch

For integration help, account provisioning, custom geoservers, or EV catalogue additions, contact BeNomad directly:

- [BeNomad website](https://www.benomad.com/)
- +33 (0)493 730 496

## Bug reports

File an issue against the [bemap-js-api repository](https://github.com/BeNomadSAS/bemap-js-api/issues) and include:

- The version (visible at the top of [Changelog](index.html#subpage-jsapi_2_0_0-changelog.md))
- The call site — URL of the demo or your own page
- Browser + console output, if available
