## Data sources

- **OpenStreetMap** — © OpenStreetMap contributors, distributed under the
  [Open Database License (ODbL)](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).
  Applies to every WMS background served by BeMap with `geoserver: 'osm'`
  and to the public BeNomad Tiles distribution (`OSM_*.pmtiles`).
- **HERE** — © HERE, distributed under HERE's commercial terms
  (<https://legal.here.com/terms>). Applies to BeMap accounts configured with
  `geoserver: 'here'` and to the internal BeNomad Tiles distribution
  (`Here-*.pmtiles`).
- **mapterhorn.com** — relief data optionally used as a raster-DEM overlay
  (hillshade / 3D terrain).
